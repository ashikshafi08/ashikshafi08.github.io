I build AI systems that work — in production, not just on paper.

My work spans LLMs, RAG pipelines, and autonomous AI, with results like:

- A bug-fixing pipeline that hit 52% on SWE-bench Verified, outperforming agent-based baselines.

- A Bittensor RAG miner generating ~$200K/month, kept online with full-stack DevOps automation.

Currently building Triage.Flow (to help developers cut through GitHub noise) and Weave (a dataset engine for more robust LLMs).

Most of what I do lives at the intersection of research and engineering. I enjoy turning ideas into systems people can run, rely on, and scale.

Stack-wise I’ve used Python, PyTorch, HuggingFace, FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, and whatever else the problem needs. But more important than the tools is the approach: build something useful, prove it works, and keep it running.

I don’t just want AI that looks good in a demo. I want AI that holds up in production.
